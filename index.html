(03) Case Studies ← lateral

The Problem.
The Process.
The Result.

Good work is easy to show. Great work is worth explaining.

Our case studies take you behind the scenes — from the challenge we set out to solve to the thinking, technology and design that brought the solution to life. Because sometimes the best part isn't just what we built. It's what it changed.

  1. Problem

    Nine hours a week, gone.

    Every booking was retyped three times across three tools. Errors were inevitable, and the person catching them was the one who should have been selling.

  2. Process

    We followed the paperwork.

    We mapped every hand-off, timed each one, and found the four moments where information actually changed shape. Only those four needed a machine.

  3. Solution

    One source. Everything downstream.

    A single record now triggers the calendar, the invoice, the confirmation and the staff brief — with a written note explaining exactly what changed and why.

  4. Result

    Nobody opens the database.

    0% fewer manual updates
    0 hours returned every week
