(03) Case Studies ← lateral

The Problem. The Process.
The Result.

Company A

Facility management · Lagos · 2026 · 38 buildings · 9 operations staff · 34 vendors · ~412 work orders a month

Modelled, not measured

170 staff hours returned a month, record errors from 7.4% to 0.3%, and the system paid back in seven months.

  1. Problem

    Nobody could say how long a job had been open.

    Work orders arrived by phone and WhatsApp, went into a notebook, and were chased from memory. Eight landlord reports were built by hand — two staff, six days a month — landing between the 8th and the 15th, with no evidence attached to anything.

  2. Process

    We followed one work order end to end.

    Every hand-off mapped and timed across 412 monthly jobs. Three of them were the same information being retyped — notebook, spreadsheet, report. Each retyping step carried its own error rate, and each one could be removed.

  3. Solution

    Raised once, on a phone, at the building.

    Routed to a vendor by trade and location, with a clock running. WhatsApp carries the deadline; reminders escalate on their own — vendor, supervisor, Head of Operations. Closing requires a photograph. On the 1st, every report assembles itself, evidenced.

  4. Result

    Nobody opens a spreadsheet.

    Supervisors work from phones; the Head of Operations works from one dashboard. Reports leave on the 1st, every month, evidenced.
